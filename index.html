    The Go Direct O₂ sensor responds to oxygen partial pressure and is factory-calibrated to read 20.9% at 1 atm. Leave calibration pressure at 1.000 unless you recalibrate the sensor at another pressure. Each logged value is the average of the Vernier readings over that minute, paired with the latest Aranet4 pressure.
